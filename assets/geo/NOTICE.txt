bd_districts.json is derived from geoBoundaries (gbOpen, BGD, ADM2,
release 9469f09), https://www.geoboundaries.org. Original data: Bangladesh
Bureau of Statistics (BBS) and OCHA ROAP, licensed CC BY 3.0 IGO.

Changes: geometry simplified and rounded, Bangla names added, and nine English
names updated to current official spellings.

bd_upazilas.json is generated by tool/build_upazila_network.py from:
- OpenStreetMap road and place data, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0,
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright (via Geofabrik).
- geoBoundaries (gbOpen, BGD, ADM3), CC BY 3.0 IGO, used to locate upazilas.
- bangladesh-geocode by Nuhil Mehdy, MIT licence, for upazila names in English
  and Bangla, https://github.com/nuhil/bangladesh-geocode.
